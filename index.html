Volunteer Management System

National Volunteer Service Papua New Guinea

The Challenge

The National Volunteer Service faced significant challenges managing volunteers across Papua New Guinea. Manual spreadsheets, no centralized data, inconsistent communication, and no way to track volunteer hours or skills efficiently. This made it nearly impossible to match the right volunteers to the right opportunities.

Our Solution

We built a comprehensive Volunteer Management System featuring centralized volunteer profiles, skills tracking, hour logging, real-time availability updates, and automatic email notifications. The system is mobile-responsive, secure, and designed for low-bandwidth access across remote provinces. Volunteer data is encrypted and protected to the highest standards.

Key Features Delivered

✓ Centralized volunteer database
✓ Skills and availability tracking
✓ Automated email integration
✓ Real-time data analytics
✓ Secure data storage
✓ Mobile-responsive design
