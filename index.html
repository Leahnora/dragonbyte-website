The team

People who build with intent.

Dragon Byte is led by two engineers committed to making secure, useful technology more accessible in Papua New Guinea.

Co-Founder / CEO

Leahnora Kedukwakwa

Leahnora is an undergraduate who has pursued cybersecurity certifications through her own initiative and independent learning outside school. Backed by two years of hands-on experience, she directs Dragon Byte’s security operations, specialising in comprehensive security audits, vulnerability assessments and risk mitigation. A recipient of multiple cybersecurity scholarships, she is dedicated to applying industry-best practices to safeguard organisational infrastructure and digital assets.

Co-Founder / CTO

Caleb Kutkue

Caleb is a graduate of Don Bosco Technological Institute (DBTI) and brings two years of specialised experience in web development and cloud computing. He oversees the end-to-end development and deployment of client projects, having launched software initiatives during his academic studies before co-founding Dragon Byte. His professional certifications in version control, project management and secure software development support the delivery of secure, scalable and highly reliable technical solutions.

Our mission

Technology should reach every corner of PNG.

Dragon Byte exists to support non-profits, NGOs and government agencies with efficient code, thoughtful design and dependable digital systems. Our passion is rural ICT development: bringing useful, accessible technology from the Highlands to the islands of Papua New Guinea through practical engineering and industry best practices.
